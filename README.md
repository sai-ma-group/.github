# Ma Lab

**Single-cell & spatial epigenomics | Technology development | Aging & disease**

We develop experimental and computational approaches to understand how
epigenetic regulation shapes cell states in development, aging, and disease.

### Research
- Single-cell DNA methylation and multi-omics
- Gene regulation
- 3D genome organization
- Aging and Alzheimer's disease
- Hematopoiesis and leukemia
- Computational genomics
