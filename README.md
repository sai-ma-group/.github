### About us

We develop experimental and computational technologies to understand how
epigenetic regulation shapes cellular identity and state transitions in
development, aging, and disease.

### Research

| | |
|---|---|
| 🧬 **Single-cell multi-omics** | DNA methylation, chromatin accessibility, Chromatin organization and transcriptomics |
| 🧠 **Aging & neurodegeneration** | Epigenetic regulation of aging and Alzheimer's disease |
| 🩸 **Hematopoiesis & leukemia** | Regulatory mechanisms underlying normal and malignant hematopoiesis |
| 🧩 **Technology development** | New single-cell and spatial epigenomic methods |
| 💻 **Computational genomics** | Methods for multimodal and regulatory genomics |
